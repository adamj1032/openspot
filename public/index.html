What hosting involves

Your driveway sits empty for most of the week. Listing it takes about a minute: drop a pin, add the address, name your price.

You control when it is open. Close the listing before guests arrive, open it again when you leave for work.

Stripe handles the money. Each fare is split at checkout and your share lands in your bank on Stripe's payout schedule.

Who you are parking with

Two checks run on every listing.

Address confirmed

Every listing carries a street address, and we check that the address sits where the host dropped the pin. Listings that fail the check still appear, marked unconfirmed, so you can decide for yourself.

Verified host

Hosts who complete Stripe payout verification have had their legal identity and bank account confirmed by Stripe. Every payout traces back to a real person.

Neither check proves who owns a property. If a listing looks wrong to you, report it and we will take it down while we investigate.
