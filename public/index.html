What hosting involves

Your driveway sits empty for most of the week. Listing it takes about a minute: drop a pin, add the address, name your price.

You control when it is open. Close the listing before guests arrive, open it again when you leave for work.

Stripe handles the money. Each fare is split at checkout and your share lands in your bank on Stripe's payout schedule.

Who you are parking with

Two checks run on every listing.

Address confirmed

Hosts type in the street address of their driveway, and we place the pin from that address rather than letting anyone drop it wherever they like. Addresses that resolve to a specific house are marked confirmed. The rest still appear, flagged as approximate, so you know to look twice when you arrive.

Verified host

Before a dollar reaches anyone, Stripe checks their identity against government ID and links a bank account in their name. Hosting on Parkeroo is not anonymous, and it cannot be.

Neither check proves who holds the deed to a house, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise. What they do is remove anonymity and leave a trail. Anyone can flag a listing, and flagged driveways come off the map while we look into them.

List your driveway

We place the pin from the address you give us, and confirm your device is at that address before the listing goes live.

Listings can only be created at the property itself. We check your device location against the address to keep people from listing driveways that are not theirs.

Write it out in full, separated by commas: number and street, town, state. Spell the street type and the state out rather than abbreviating them.
Example: 123 Main Street, Anytown, New Jersey

Take it now, from the driveway itself: a clear view of where a car would pull in. Drivers see this photo before they commit, and it is the main reason a spot gets picked.

Need the driveway back by a certain time each day? Nobody can book past it. Leave blank for no limit.

Drivers say how long they need, and anything longer than this is refused automatically. You are never asked to approve a booking, so nobody waits on you.

Kept private until a car is booked in, so a gate code or door detail is safe here. Good instructions are the main reason drivers do not need to message you.
